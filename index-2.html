The Studio

Not everything needs to be posed.

Dream Frames started with a simple idea: the best photo of the day is rarely the one everyone lined up for. We shoot weddings and personal sessions the same way — stay close, stay quiet, and let the real moment happen in front of the lens.

Every shoot includes a same-week preview gallery, and full edited sets are delivered within the timeline agreed at booking.
